Chronic obstructive pulmonary disease (COPD)

Disease description

A disease of chronic diffuse irreversible airflow obstruction. Subcategories of COPD include CHRONIC BRONCHITIS and PULMONARY EMPHYSEMA. Year introduced: 2002

Source: MeSH
